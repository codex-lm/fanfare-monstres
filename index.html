How to play

Notes fall down four lanes. Press the key of the matching monster exactly when the note reaches its pad.

Perfect timing = full points. A chain of hits raises the multiplier (up to ×4), wakes up the audience and lights the stage. A miss breaks the chain — but the music keeps going!

The piece is a success above 60% accuracy. On touch screens, tap the lane itself.
